Fail-safe by design. One fix commit per PR per run, nothing pushed that wasn't validated, no pushes after an approval, never merges or pings maintainers unasked.
Knows its own work. Its commits carry the cluster's SSH signature and an Automated-by: pr-shepherd trailer, so they never trigger a new review.
